### Label behaviour

* Show percentage labels inside slices only when they fit comfortably.
* For small slices where the percentage does not fit, draw a thin leader line from the slice to an external percentage label.
* The leader line should:

  1. Start at the outer edge of the slice.
  2. Extend slightly outward.
  3. Bend horizontally toward the label.
* Place labels on the left or right according to the slice’s midpoint.
* Keep the label close to its slice so the relationship remains obvious.

### Prevent labels from leaving the screen

Calculate all positions using the chart container’s actual dimensions, not the viewport or fixed pixel coordinates.

* Reserve safe space around the pie for external labels.
* Reduce the pie radius slightly on mobile to create this space.
* Keep every line and label within the chart card’s left and right padding.
* Clamp calculated label positions to the container boundaries.
* Never allow text to be clipped, hidden or positioned off-screen.
* Recalculate positions whenever the screen size or orientation changes.

### Prevent overlapping labels

Handle labels separately on the left and right sides:

* Sort external labels vertically.
* Maintain sufficient vertical spacing between them.
* Shift overlapping labels up or down.
* Redraw each leader line to the label’s final adjusted position.
* Keep labels readable at a minimum of 12px.
* Do not make text extremely small to force it into a slice.

Use a sensible internal-label threshold, approximately 8%, but determine whether a label fits using the slice’s actual available space as well.

### Guaranteed visibility

Every category’s percentage must always appear in at least one guaranteed location:

* Inside the slice when it fits.
* Beside the pie with a leader line when it does not.
* Also in the legend below the chart with the category name, percentage and amount.

If there are too many tiny slices to place all external labels without collisions, prioritize clean leader-line labels for the available space and rely on the complete legend for the remaining values. Never place labels beyond the card.

### Responsive chart

* Use a responsive SVG viewBox or the chart library’s responsive dimensions.
* Recalculate the pie radius, centroids, callout positions and collision spacing with a `ResizeObserver`.
* Keep the pie centred after space is reserved for labels.
* On tap or hover, show the category, percentage and currency amount in a tooltip within the card.
* Preserve the existing data, colours, filters and calculations.

---
name: Neutral appearance system
description: The app's visual direction and appearance-setting constraint.
---

Split Mate's public/auth branding and logo use a neutral palette: black, graphite, gray, and white. Keep decorative split-mark strokes inside the S outline.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a black-and-gray landing page and logo, and the prior split-mark seam visibly crossed the letterform border.

**How to apply:** Use grayscale for public/auth backgrounds, logo artwork, and app/splash icons. Preserve semantic colors when they communicate status rather than branding.
